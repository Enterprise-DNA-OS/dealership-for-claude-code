description: The deals board - quotes, deposits held, recent deliveries with front-end gross, and anything going stale. The sales meeting screen.
---

1. Run `node scripts/dealer.mjs deals --json`.
2. Present by state: deposits first (call out DEPOSIT STALE with days), then quotes, then the last 28 days of deliveries with gross.
3. For each stale deposit, the action is a phone call today; `note add` records what was said.
